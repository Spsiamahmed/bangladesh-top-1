## 📌 About The Project

This is the **official website** of **Bangladesh TOP 1 (BD Top 1)** , the premier Free Fire esports team in Bangladesh, led by **Md Siam**. The website showcases the team's achievements, roster, tournament highlights, and provides a hub for the Bangladeshi Free Fire gaming community.

### 🎯 Purpose

- Establish **Md Siam** as the #1 Free Fire player in Bangladesh
- Promote the **Bangladesh TOP 1** esports brand
- Connect with fans, sponsors, and the gaming community
- Showcase tournament wins and gameplay highlights
- Provide easy access to UID, social media, and contact information
